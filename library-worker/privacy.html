Privacy Policy

Oversized Parking · Effective November 8, 2026

Oversized Parking helps drivers of tall vehicles find parking that fits. You don't need an account to use it. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have.

Your location

If you allow it, the app uses your device's location to show parking near you. Your exact location stays on your phone. To load parking data, the app downloads map tiles for your area; each tile covers roughly 17 by 14 miles, so our server only learns which broad area is being viewed. We don't store a history of where you've been.

If you type a place to search, the text is sent to a geocoding service (your phone's built-in service, or OpenStreetMap's Nominatim) to turn it into a map position.

Things you save and report

  • Your vehicles (My garage), trips, saved spots, notes and phone numbers you add are stored only on your phone.
  • Shared reports. If you share a clearance, price or note about a parking spot, it is stored on our database (Supabase) with a random anonymous ID, so other drivers can see the community summary. Reports aren't linked to your name, email or phone number. You can delete a report you shared from the spot's page.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers (such as your phone's advertising ID), approximate location from your IP address, and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. On iPhone, ads are personalized only if you allow tracking when asked; in regions that require it, you're asked for consent first. You can change these choices at any time in your phone's settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Sponsored listings: we count how often a sponsored listing is shown or tapped, without recording who saw it.

Purchases

Oversized Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Apple or Google. We use RevenueCat to confirm your purchase and restore it on a new phone; it receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase receipt, not your payment details.

Parking data

Parking information comes from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL), our own research of public sources, and reports shared by drivers. Height clearances can change and signs can be wrong. Always check the posted clearance before entering.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • We don't ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • The app isn't directed at children under 13.

Your choices and rights

You can turn off location access, decline ad tracking, delete reports you've shared, or delete the app to remove everything stored on your phone. Depending on where you live (for example California or the EU), you may have rights to access or delete information about you. Contact us and we'll help.
