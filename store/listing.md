## Description (both stores, under 4,000 characters)

Driving something tall? Stop guessing whether you'll fit.

Oversized Parking shows parking garages and lots near you or your destination, checked against your vehicle's height. Set your height once — lifted pickup, Sprinter van, truck with a roof rack, camper — and every result tells you at a glance:

• FITS — the posted clearance is above your height with room to spare
• TIGHT — within a few inches, take it slow
• TOO LOW — skip it
• UNKNOWN — no clearance on record, with tips and a one-tap call button

FIND PARKING THAT ACTUALLY WORKS
• Map and list views, sorted by distance, best fit or free first
• Open-air lots, which have no height limit, clearly marked
• Prices, hours, capacity and payment details where available
• "Public only" filter hides customer-only and private lots so you don't get towed
• A mini map with satellite view and Street View link for every location
• One-tap directions in Apple Maps or Google Maps

HEIGHTS YOU CAN TRUST
Clearances come from OpenStreetMap, our own research of operator and city websites, and drivers like you. Every researched height links to its source. If you know a clearance, share it in seconds — your report helps the next driver.

MY GARAGE: EVERY VEHICLE YOU DRIVE
Add your pickup, van, RV or truck-and-trailer with its height and length, and switch between them in one tap. Results re-check instantly.

PLAN TRIPS AHEAD
Line up the garages and lots you'll stay at, in order, with nights at each. See your dates, which stops fit the vehicle you're taking, and get directions for the whole route.

CALL AHEAD
Garage phone numbers where available, one-tap calling, and a quick search when a number isn't on record.

SAVE YOUR GO-TO SPOTS
Save garages and lots you use, add your own notes, and find them again fast.

OVERSIZED PRO — ONE PAYMENT, NO SUBSCRIPTION
Free with ads. Upgrade once to remove ads and get unlimited vehicles, trips and saved spots.

Always check the posted clearance sign before you enter. Heights can change and signs can be wrong.

Parking data © OpenStreetMap contributors.

## Apple "App Privacy" answers

Data used to track you: **Device ID** (advertising identifier, only if the user
allows tracking), **Advertising data**.

Data linked to you: **None**.

Data not linked to you:
- Location → Coarse location (approximate area of map tiles requested;
  AdMob may infer from IP) — App functionality, Third-party advertising
- Identifiers → Device ID — Third-party advertising
- Usage data → Advertising data, Product interaction (sponsored listing
  views/taps) — Third-party advertising, Analytics
- Purchases → Purchase history (RevenueCat, anonymous ID) — App functionality
- User content → Other user content (shared clearance/price reports) — App functionality
- Diagnostics → none collected by us

## Google Play "Data safety" answers

- Data collected: Approximate location; Device or other IDs; App interactions;
  Purchase history; Other user-generated content.
- Shared with third parties: Device or other IDs, approximate location and app
  interactions with Google AdMob for advertising.
- Encrypted in transit: Yes. Users can request deletion: Yes (delete shared
  reports in the app; contact for anything else).
- Precise location: used on device only and not sent to us — answer "not collected".
